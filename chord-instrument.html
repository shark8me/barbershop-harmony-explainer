Chord Instrument

3 rows — subdominant / tonic / dominant — × chord columns. Powered by Strudel's audio engine.
tap a pad to play
How it works: each row is a harmonic position in the key — tonic, subdominant (a 4th above / 5th below), dominant (a 5th above). Each column is a chord shape applied to that row's root, so the pad you press always sounds in key. The +2 column gives the chord a whole step above the tonic row (the tritone lead note's chord, e.g. D7 in C); −2 gives the ♭VII side (the flat-7 lead note's chord, e.g. B♭ in C).
